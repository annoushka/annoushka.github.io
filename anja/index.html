so my name is anja. i'm 33 years old. i am a certified health and wellness coach. passionate about everything related to nature and vitality, but most importantly about connecting with the people with whom i work. i have such a gratitude, that’s such an honor that people are entrusting me their dreams, hopes, and so much more when we’re working together. i try my best so we get to feel safe, inspired, and connected. my approach is to push clients to take ownership of their journey relatively early and remain cause focused.

working with clients made me realize that the missing piece is most likely self knowledge so this guide is built for self reflection and increasing awareness around what needs your attention for your personal healing and growth

i became a certified health and wellness coach because i lost my mother quite young and am very cognizant of taking care of my health. her health was always a big concern and issue for our family and since we know that about 90% of our health is determined by lifestyle i find it critical to prioritize mindfulness around our routines and habits that reinforce our way of caring for ourselves every day.

my philosophy on wellness arises from my personal experience of childhood trauma and grief affecting my health and stalling my thriving. i believe that elevating your energy levels and experience of life begins with cultivating mindset that prioritizes true well-being of self and everything around. becoming healthier as an individual transforms communities and creates a stronger healthier environment which has a ripple effect

one of the reasons i have been drawn to health coaching is my own transformation from an obese kid to losing 40kg at 16 to starting training for athleticism at 32 without any sports background
i grew up obese, and at 16 | started to feel some autonomy and agency
that was the moment i decided to become fit, and lost over 40 kg
years later, at 31, i wanted to support my health with more movement
i tried yoga, pilates, and lifting weights like a bodybuilder, and only when i added jumping and sprint training it really clicked
and i recalled that at school i was pretty good at short distances and i was the last one on long runs my body seems to thrive on explosive work, and it gives me real joy along the way, i also built muscle on a plant-based diet — not by aiming for it, but as a natural result of training and i am welcoming any body adaptations on my journey to becoming fast and powerful
what i try to bring into my coaching is that same sense of ownership and self-reflection: gently helping people notice what actually works for them, not what they think they're supposed to do
